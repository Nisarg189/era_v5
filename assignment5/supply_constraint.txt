agentic         63B             0.7%   this is the hard limit on the lane
------------------------------------------------------------------

  Web and code can supply more than an entire 9T run on their own, so they
  never constrain the mixture. The four scarce lanes each have a real ceiling,
  and those ceilings are what the mixture has to be built around.

  The ceilings are fixed in tokens, so a larger budget would not raise them.
  It would only lower each scarce lane's reachable share and push the freed
  space into general web, which is the one lane with room to spare.

  The plan checked against these ceilings is in plan_arithmetic.py.
