WHAT BUDGET THIS SUPPORTS

  The ceiling for each scarce lane, expressed as the largest share of a run
  of a given size that the lane can actually fill:

  budget       web      code     indic      stem    reason   agentic
--------------------------------------------------------------------
     15T   127.8%    29.4%     8.4%     7.2%     3.6%     0.4%
     12T   159.7%    36.8%    10.5%     9.0%     4.5%     0.5%
      9T   212.9%    49.0%    14.0%    12.0%     6.0%     0.7%
      8T   239.6%    55.1%    15.7%    13.5%     6.8%     0.8%
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  Read the Indic column. At a 15T budget the Indic lane cannot exceed 8.4%
  however it is arranged, because that is all the Indic text there is to
  download. Everything the mixture cannot fill with a scarce lane it must
  fill with general web, which is the one lane with room to spare. A 15T
  budget therefore forces the web-heavy mixture this session warns against,
  not by choice but by arithmetic.

  Shrinking the budget raises every scarce lane's reachable share, because
  the ceiling is fixed in tokens while the denominator falls.
